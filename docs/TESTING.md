# Phone Tester Checklist v2 — L to Local ("Keep Left")

Use this checklist on an Android phone (for example OnePlus 12).
If something fails, write one line: `Screen / Lesson | What you tapped | What happened`.

## 1. Controls, Steering & Audio
- [ ] **Steering wheel & pedals:** Steer left/right with the redesigned wheel, accelerate, and brake smoothly in both right-hand-drive seat view and chase view.
- [ ] **Indicators:** Tap the left/right indicator buttons above the wheel and brake; confirm clear click (`indicator_on.wav` / `indicator_off.wav`).
- [ ] **Aim assist & mirrors:** Check aim assist feels natural in corners and live rear-view + door mirrors update smoothly.
- [ ] **Audio & `.tip` coach lines:** Listen for smooth engine loops (`engine_idle.mp3` -> `engine_low.wav` -> `engine_mid.mp3` -> `engine_high.wav`) and verify a short `.tip` line plays if you fail the same rule twice.

## 2. Theory Test Mode
- [ ] **10 mock tests:** Open Theory Tests, start any set (`set-01` to `set-10`), and confirm 50 questions, 57-minute timer, and 43/50 pass mark.
- [ ] **Bug check:** Any question with missing options or cut-off text -> log set number and question ID.

## 3. Core Stages (0–7) & New Rules
- [ ] **Stage 0 (Free, Lessons 1–3):** Keep left, MSM moving off, 20/30/40 mph speed limits.
- [ ] **Stage 1 (Turning & Signalling, Lesson 29):** Signal early before left/right turns and wait for safe gaps when turning right.
- [ ] **Stage 1 (STOP & Give Way, Lesson 4):** Come to a complete stop at the solid white line at STOP junctions; yield at broken give-way lines.
- [ ] **Stage 2 (Lights & Crossings, Lessons 7–10):** Stop on red/amber, keep cycle boxes and yellow box junctions clear, stop for zebra crossings, never overtake on zig-zags.
- [ ] **Stage 3 (Roundabouts, Lessons 11–14):** Give way to the right, pick approach lane early, stay in lane, and signal left to exit.
- [ ] **Stage 4 (School Zones, Lesson 28):** Slow down past school warning signs and watch for pedestrians near the school gate.
- [ ] **Stage 4 (Coventry Ring Road, Lesson 32):** Run all 6 short Coventry practice drives (`ring lanes`, `roundabout`, `signals`, `pedestrians`, `speed change`, `give way`).
- [ ] **Stages 5–7 (Dual Carriageways, Motorways, Conditions):** Check 2-second gap at speed, Red X / variable gantry limits, and night/rain/fog lights & wipers.

## 4. Difficulty, Variants & Mock Tests
- [ ] **Difficulty ladder:** Confirm each lesson starts easy (`1`) and ramps smoothly without sudden spikes.
- [ ] **Replay variants (`_b` / `_c`):** Open Lessons 2, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 28, 29 and try a `_b` or `_c` replay variant; confirm stars are not blocked by variants.
- [ ] **Mock Driving Tests (Stage 8, Lessons 19–20, 30–31):** Complete a Wolverhampton test-area mock drive and a Coventry mock drive; verify debrief replays and fault counts.
