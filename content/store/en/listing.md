## Full description (<= 4000 characters)
**L to Local (Keep Left)** helps UK learner drivers and newcomers to Great Britain learn UK road rules by driving, not just reading.

Drive low-poly 3D courses and get instant, plain-English feedback linked to the official Highway Code.

### What is included today
- **4 playable 3D courses:** a town-centre course, a two-lane roundabout, a single-lane roundabout, and a 40 mph dual carriageway. <!-- claim: courses_four -->
- **Live Highway Code feedback:** speed limits, lane changes, roundabouts, overtaking cyclists (1.5 m), following distance (2 s), and town-centre junction rules. <!-- claim: live_rules -->
- **Realistic UK driving view:** right-hand-drive seat view, chase view, live rear-view and door mirrors, and an optional look over your shoulder. <!-- claim: cameras_mirrors -->
- **End-of-drive debrief:** review your driving, serious, and dangerous faults with replays and a practice pass or fail verdict. <!-- claim: debrief_replays -->
- **Settings:** adjust steering sensitivity to suit your style. <!-- claim: settings_screen -->
- **Offline by design:** no account required, and your settings stay on your device. <!-- claim: offline_device -->

### Coming soon
- **18 structured lessons (Stages 0–5):** first 3 lessons free. <!-- claim: lessons_18 -->
- **Wolverhampton (beta):** real-streets free drive built from OpenStreetMap data. <!-- claim: wolverhampton_beta -->
- **Practise this lesson:** jump straight from a debrief fault to the matching lesson. <!-- claim: practise_this_lesson -->
