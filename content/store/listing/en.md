## Full description (<= 4000 characters)
**L to Local** helps UK learners and newcomers to Great Britain aged 18+ practise UK road rules by driving in 3D.

Every drive gives you instant, plain-English feedback linked to the Highway Code, so you learn what went wrong and why right away. Try the first 3 lessons free, or unlock everything for £14.99 one-off.

### What is included
- **32 structured lessons:** practise keeping left, speed limits, school zones, STOP signs, turning signals, traffic lights, zebra crossings, box junctions, roundabouts, overtaking cyclists, night/rain/fog driving, and motorways.
- **10 mock theory tests:** 50 practice questions per test, 43 to pass, and a 57-minute timer.
- **Instant Highway Code feedback:** clear messages while you drive, with no confusing jargon.
- **Debrief and replays:** review your driving faults, serious faults, and dangerous faults after every drive, and replay key moments.
- **Right-hand-drive UK view:** drive from the right-hand seat or chase view with live mirrors.

### Important notice
L to Local is an independent practice and learning aid for adults aged 18+. It is not a substitute for lessons with an approved driving instructor (ADI). This app is not endorsed by or affiliated with DVSA or DfT, does not use official DVSA test material, and does not guarantee a driving test pass.
