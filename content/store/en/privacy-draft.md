# Privacy Notice Draft (v1)

**DRAFT: owner and legal review needed**

## Overview
**L to Local** ("Keep Left") is designed to work offline in v1.

## Data we collect in v1
- **No user accounts:** You do not sign in or provide an email address, name, or phone number.
- **No analytics or tracking:** v1 does not send telemetry, crash reports, or usage analytics off your device.
- **Local storage only:** Your steering sensitivity setting, lesson progress, and practice fault history are stored locally on your device. You can clear them at any time from Settings.
