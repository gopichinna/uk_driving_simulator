# ADI Review: Game Judging Thresholds (`PROPOSED` & `ASSUMPTION`)

This table lists **every numeric threshold** currently implemented in `core/Rules.Core/*.cs` or proposed in `docs/specs/next-rules*.md` where the Highway Code does not specify a fixed number (or where a tolerance/time window is a simulator assumption). No values have been changed here; each row includes a specific question for the Approved Driving Instructor (ADI).

| Rule ID | Highway Code | Value | Unit | Plain-English Behaviour | Why Chosen | Question for ADI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GB.SPD.OVER_LIMIT` | Rule 124 | `0.5` | mph | Tolerance above posted speed limit before flagging. | Avoids false faults from tiny dial/cruise fluctuations. | Is +0.5 mph fair, or do examiners allow +1–2 mph briefly? |
| `GB.SPD.OVER_LIMIT` | Rule 124 | `1.5` | s | How long speed must stay over limit before flagging. | Filters brief downhill overshoots when lifting off. | Is 1.5 s right, or should any sustained excess count? |
| `GB.SPD.OVER_LIMIT` | Rule 124 | `15` (`10` in 20 mph) | mph over | Excess speed that escalates `Serious` to `Dangerous`. | High excess speed leaves almost no stopping margin. | Does +10 mph in a 20 zone / +15 mph elsewhere match test marking? |
| `GB.GAP.TWO_SECOND` | Rule 126 | `20` | mph | Minimum speed above which the 2-second gap rule applies. | Stops false tailgating faults in slow stop-start queues. | Is 20 mph the right cut-off for moving traffic? |
| `GB.GAP.TWO_SECOND` | Rule 126 | `1.0` | s | Headway below which a `Serious` fault escalates to `Dangerous`. | Under 1 s leaves less than typical reaction time. | Should < 1.0 s be `Dangerous` even at 20–30 mph? |
| `GB.GAP.TWO_SECOND` | Rule 126 | `3.0` / `2.0` | s | Sustain time (`MinorAfterS = 3.0`, `SeriousAfterS = 2.0`) before flagging gap fault (`ASSUMPTION`). | Filters momentary cut-ins by other traffic. | Are 3 s minor / 2 s serious sustain windows fair? |
| `GB.OBS.LANE_CHANGE` | Rule 133 | `4.0` | m | Longitudinal gap (`DangerousGapM`) below which a lane-change cut-in is `Dangerous`. | Under ~1 car length risks immediate side/rear contact. | What minimum clearance in metres/seconds feels realistic at 30–70 mph? |
| `GB.OBS.LANE_CHANGE` | Rule 133 | `1.5` / `3.0` | s | `MinSignalBeforeS = 1.5`, `ConflictWindowS = 3.0`, `TooCloseAheadS = 1.0` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Checks signal lead and post-move conflict window. | Is a 1.5 s signal lead enough before changing lanes? |
| `GB.OBS.LANE_CHANGE` | Rule 133 | `3.0` / `6.0` | m/s² | `SeriousBrakeMps2 = 3.0`, `DangerousBrakeMps2 = 6.0` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Measures how hard the affected vehicle had to brake. | Do 3.0 / 6.0 m/s² match Serious vs Dangerous? |
| `GB.OBS.LANE_CHANGE` | Rule 133 | `8.0` / `0.35` | m | `BlindSpotBehindM = 8.0`, `LaneHysteresisM = 0.35` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Models blind-spot zone and steering jitter buffer. | Does 8 m behind cover typical motorway blind spots? |
| `GB.SIG.TIMELY` | Rule 103 | `80.0` | m | Radius (`SignalNeededWithinM`) within which another road user must be present to require a signal. | Matches "if anyone could benefit" without busywork on empty roads. | Is 80 m a good radius for needing a signal in town and dual carriageways? |
| `GB.POS.KEEP_LEFT` | Rule 160 | `13.0` | m | Radius (`JunctionRadiusM`) around a junction centre where lane-position check is paused during turns. | Prevents false wrong-side faults while cutting across a T-junction turn. | Is 13 m appropriate for UK town junctions? |
| `GB.POS.KEEP_LEFT` | Rule 160 | `0.5` / `1.0` / `0.7` | m / s / conf | `IntoLaneM = 0.5`, `SeriousAfterS = 1.0`, `MinConfidence = 0.7` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Distinguishes minor drift from sustained wrong-side driving on high-confidence links. | Is > 0.5 m across the centre line for 1.0 s fair for `Serious`? |
| `GB.POS.KEEP_LEFT` | Rule 160 | `60.0` | m | Distance (`OncomingWithinM`) to oncoming car that escalates wrong-side driving to `Dangerous`. | Gives ~2–3 s closing time at 30 mph. | Should oncoming presence escalate at 60 m or further (e.g. 100 m)? |
| `GB.RBT.SIGNAL_EXIT` | Rule 186 | `0.25` | rad (~4 m) | Angle before exit where exit signal check applies. | Ensures player signals after passing the previous exit. | Is ~4 m before the exit diverge realistic for signalling left? |
| `GB.TL.AMBER` | Rule 175 | `3.0` | m/s² | `ComfortDecelMps2 = 3.0` used to decide if player can stop safely on amber (`ASSUMPTION`). | Avoids forcing emergency braking when amber appears late. | Is 3.0 m/s² comfortable deceleration realistic for amber stops? |
| `GB.JCT.GIVE_WAY` | Rule 172 | `1.5` / `3.0` / `6.0` | m/s² | `MinorDecelMps2 = 1.5`, `SeriousDecelMps2 = 3.0`, `DangerousDecelMps2 = 6.0`, `WindowS = 10` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Judges emerging priority by how hard major-road traffic brakes. | Do 1.5 / 3.0 / 6.0 m/s² over 10 s match give-way fault severity? |
| `GB.SCH.SLOW` | Rule 208 | `100` / `15` / `1.5` | m / m / s | `RadiusM = 100`, `PeopleM = 15`, `SustainS = 1.5` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Covers sign zone, nearby pedestrian escalation, and brief throttle lift-off. | Is 100 m around the sign and 15 m for pedestrians right? |
| `GB.SCH.SLOW` | Rule 208 | `20` | mph | Target speed cap (`LimitMph`) inside a school zone (`ASSUMPTION`). | Common UK 20 mph school-zone speed where signed; Rule 208 says "drive slowly". | Should 20 mph be the pass cap on all school zones, or only when children/parked cars are present? |
| `GB.JCT.TURN_NEAR` | Rules 103, 182 | `2.0` / `0.7` / `0.17` / `14` | s / rad / rad / m | `SignalLeadS = 2.0`, `TurnRad = 0.7` (~40° turn), `TurnStartRad = 0.17`, `RadiusM = 14` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Detects genuine junction left turns and checks 2 s prior signal. | Is 2.0 s lead time and 0.7 rad turn angle right for town left turns? |
| `GB.JCT.TURN_FAR_GAP` | Rule 180 | `3.5` / `1.5` | s | `SeriousTtcS = 3.5`, `DangerousTtcS = 1.5` to oncoming traffic (`ASSUMPTION`). | Typical 2.5–3 s turn completion plus margin so oncoming cars do not brake. | Is 3.5 s Serious / 1.5 s Dangerous realistic on 30–40 mph roads? |
| `GB.JCT.TURN_FAR_GAP` | Rule 180 | `70` / `2.0` / `4.0` / `0.35` / `14` | m / m/s² / rad | `LookAheadM = 70`, `SeriousDecelMps2 = 2.0`, `DangerousDecelMps2 = 4.0`, `TurnStartRad = 0.35`, `RadiusM = 14` (`ASSUMPTION`). | Scans 70 m ahead for oncoming cars and checks induced braking. | Is 70 m look-ahead enough on faster 40–50 mph roads? |
| `GB.JCT.STOP_SIGN` | Rule 171 | `0.2` / `6` | m/s / m | `StoppedMps = 0.2`, `ApproachM = 6` behind STOP line (`PROPOSED` / `ASSUMPTION`). | Allows for tiny physics settle within 6 m without allowing rolling stops. | Does < 0.2 m/s (~0.4 mph) feel like a true full stop? |
| `GB.JCT.BOX` | Rule 174 | `2.0` | s | `StoppedForS = 2.0` inside yellow box before flagging (`ASSUMPTION`). | Distinguishes creeping traffic from stopping inside the box. | Is 2.0 s stopped inside a yellow box the right threshold? |
| `GB.POS.DUAL_KEEP_LEFT` | Rule 137 | `10.0` | s | Time in lane 1+ with lane 0 clear (`40 m` ahead, `100 m` from right turn) before flagging (`PROPOSED`). | Avoids penalising brief overtakes while catching lane hogging. | Is 10 s too generous or about right for middle-lane hogging? |
| `GB.OBS.MOVE_OFF_CHECKS` | Rule 159 | `1.5` / `3.0` | m/s² | Required deceleration of passing car (`> 1.5` minor, `> 3.0` serious) within `3.0 s` (`PROPOSED`). | Judges outcome ("did you make them brake?") instead of mirror-tap busywork. | Do 1.5 / 3.0 m/s² match "causing another road user to slow down vs brake hard"? |
| `GB.OBS.MSM` | Rule 161 | `1.5` | s | Minimum signal lead time before turning or sharp braking (`> 3.5 m/s²`, `PROPOSED`). | Mirrors Rule 161 timing without mirror-button busywork. | Is 1.5 s acceptable for slowing/stopping, vs 2.0 s for turns? |
| `GB.SPD.APPROPRIATE` | Rule 125 | `0.85 × limit` | mph | Hazard-adjusted speed cap within `25.0 m` of parked cars with `< 1.5 m` clearance (`PROPOSED`). | Encourages ~25 mph or less in tight 30 mph streets. | What speed do you expect past a row of parked cars with poor visibility? |
| `GB.GAP.WET_ICY` | Rule 227 | `4.0` | s | Minimum following gap in rain (`< 2.0 s` escalates to `Serious`, `PROPOSED`). | Highway Code Rule 227: stopping distances are at least doubled in wet weather. | Is a strict 4.0 s gap the right target in simulated rain? |
| `GB.WX.FOG_STOPPING` | Rule 235 | `0.8 × vis` | m | Maximum stopping distance (`v²/(2×4.5) + v×1.0`) relative to fog visibility (`PROPOSED`). | Ensures driver can pull up well within clear sight distance. | Is 80% of fog visibility with 4.5 m/s² decel realistic? |
| `GB.VRU.PASS_HORSE` | Rule 215 | `10` / `2.0` | mph / m | Max speed and min lateral space (statutory Rule 215); `> 20 mph` or `< 1.0 m` = `Dangerous` (`PROPOSED`). | Base numbers come directly from Highway Code Rule 215; escalation is proposed. | Do the `Dangerous` escalation cut-offs (> 20 mph or < 1 m) match test standards? |
