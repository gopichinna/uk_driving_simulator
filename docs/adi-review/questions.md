# ADI Review: Open Questions Collected Across Rounds

1. **School-zone speed assumption (`GB.SCH.SLOW`, Round 34 & 36):** Highway Code Rule 208 says "drive slowly" near schools without stating a number. Is our `20 mph` cap within `100 m` of a school warning sign a fair teaching rule, or should `25 mph` be allowed when no children/pedestrians are present?
2. **Turning signal & gap thresholds (`GB.JCT.TURN_NEAR`, `GB.JCT.TURN_FAR_GAP`, Round 36):** Are a `2.0 s` left-signal lead time and a `3.5 s` oncoming gap before turning right realistic for a UK practical driving test in 30 mph and 40 mph zones?
3. **Outcome-based observation (`AGENTS.md` §4.7, `GB.OBS.MSM`, `GB.OBS.MOVE_OFF_CHECKS`):** Because mobile touch controls omit mirror/shoulder-check buttons, the simulator judges outcomes (did another vehicle have to brake or swerve?). Does this teach good habits if paired with coach voice reminders, or are there specific manoeuvres where an explicit observation prompt is essential?
4. **Amber-light dilemma zone (`GB.TL.AMBER`):** At 30 mph, what stopping deceleration (`4.5 m/s²` proposed) best separates "safe to stop on amber" from "too close, continue safely"?
5. **Rain & fog gap teaching (`GB.GAP.WET_ICY`, `GB.WX.FOG_STOPPING`):** In Stage 7 rain scenarios, should a `3.5–4.0 s` gap be strictly enforced (4 s = doubled 2 s rule per Rule 227), or treated as `Driving` fault at `< 4 s` and `Serious` at `< 2 s`?
6. **4-exit asymmetric roundabouts (`docs/specs/roundabout-4-exit-build.md`, Queslett `way=792`):** When an exit sits around `165°` from entry (slightly before/after 12 o'clock), should both approach lanes be accepted when road markings are absent, or only the left lane unless arrows state otherwise?
7. **STOP junctions (`GB.JCT.STOP_SIGN`, Rule 171):** How long must wheels remain stationary (`< 0.2 m/s`) behind the solid line — is any true zero-speed stop sufficient, or should a `1.0 s` pause be expected before creeping forward for sightline?
