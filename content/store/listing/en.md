L to Local: UK Driving

## Short description (<= 80 characters)
Practise UK road rules in 3D with instant Highway Code feedback.

## Full description (<= 4000 characters)
**L to Local** helps UK learners and newcomers to Great Britain practise UK road rules by driving in 3D.

Every drive gives you instant, plain-English feedback linked to the official Highway Code, so you learn what went wrong and why right away.

### What is included
- **Town, Wolverhampton, and motorway lessons:** practise keeping left, traffic lights, zebra crossings, box junctions, roundabouts, overtaking cyclists, night/rain/fog driving, and motorways.
- **Instant Highway Code feedback:** clear messages while you drive, with no confusing jargon.
- **Debrief and replays:** review your driving faults, serious faults, and dangerous faults after every drive, and replay key moments.
- **Review drives and final mock test:** practise weak rules on varied drives, then try a full mock driving test.
- **Right-hand-drive UK view:** drive from the right-hand seat or chase view with live mirrors.

### Important notice
L to Local is a practice and learning aid. It is not a substitute for lessons with an approved driving instructor (ADI). This app is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with DVSA or DfT.
