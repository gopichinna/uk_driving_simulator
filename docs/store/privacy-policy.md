## 1. Overview
**L to Local** ("we", "our") is an offline-first UK driving rules practice app. We respect your privacy under the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**.

## 2. What Data We Collect
In the current offline version of the app, **we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers**. There are no user accounts, no advertisements, and no third-party analytics SDKs.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Phone
To remember your preferences and lesson progress, the app saves simple settings locally on your device using Android/iOS app storage (`PlayerPrefs`):
- Your steering sensitivity, sound, and voice settings
- Which lessons and review drives you have completed

This data stays on your phone and is never sent to us or any third party.

## 4. How to Delete Your Data
You can delete all saved progress at any time by:
1. Using the reset option inside the app, or
2. Clearing the app's storage in your phone's Settings, or
3. Uninstalling the app.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Open Data
The app includes pre-packaged map and road-rule data from **OpenStreetMap** (ODbL-1.0) and **GOV.UK / The Highway Code & DfT Traffic Sign Images** (Open Government Licence v3.0). Using those offline files does not send any data to OpenStreetMap or GOV.UK.

## 6. Children
L to Local is designed for learner drivers aged **18 and over**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
